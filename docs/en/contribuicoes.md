## Internal division

The division follows the same principle adopted in the previous project:
each member owns one technical focus area, but everyone must know the full
solution and be able to explain any part of it during the presentation, as
required by the final project instructions.

## Workflow

Contributions must follow the flow described in
[fluxo-git.md](fluxo-git.md): a feature branch off `develop`, an MR reviewed
by another member and, once the integrated version in `develop` is
validated, a final MR from `develop` to `main`.
